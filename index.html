About Me

I like AI that has to work under pressure. At the National Aerospace Science & Technology Park (NASTP), that meant building an offline speech-recognition system for fighter jet cockpits — no cloud, no second chances, just clean transcription in real time. Since then I've carried that same "make it actually work" mindset into large language models and multi-agent systems: platforms that validate planning applications, rewrite resumes for ATS, and recommend stock decisions from live market events.

My background is mathematics and data science, so I'm as comfortable in the theory as I am in the deployment weeds — dataset engineering, model optimization, and gluing it all together into pipelines that don't fall over. What I want next is a team building AI products people actually rely on.

If that sounds like your team, let's talk.
